Web Design FAQ

Everything you need to know about working with AjayaDesign — pricing, process, AI automation, and support.

💰 Pricing & Packages

Our websites range from $500 to $2,500+, depending on complexity. A single-page landing site starts around $500, a standard multi-page business site runs $800–$1,500, and custom builds with AI automation, e-commerce, or advanced features start at $2,000+. Every quote is transparent — no hidden fees.

Every package includes responsive mobile-first design, on-page SEO optimization, Lighthouse 95+ performance scores, Google Analytics 4 setup, contact form integration, SSL/HTTPS, and a 24-hour live preview before you commit.

We require 50% upfront to begin work, with the remaining 50% due upon delivery and your approval. For projects over $2,000, we offer three-payment plans. We accept all major credit cards, Zelle, and bank transfers.

⚡ Process & Timeline

Within 24 hours of submitting your intake form and deposit, you'll receive a live preview link with your site running on a real URL — not a mockup or PDF, but an actual working website you can click through.

Most projects are completed in 1–2 weeks from kickoff to launch. Simple sites can go live in under a week. Complex builds with custom AI automation or e-commerce may take 2–3 weeks. We'll give you a specific timeline before starting.

Every project includes 2 rounds of revisions at no extra cost. Most clients need only one. Additional revision rounds are available at $150 each, but honestly, our 24-hour preview process means we usually nail it early.

Fill out our contact form or intake form with your business details, goals, and any design preferences. We'll review it, send you a quote within 24 hours, and once you approve and submit your deposit, your 24-hour preview clock starts.

🔧 Technical

We default to GitHub Pages — it's free, fast, and includes SSL. For clients who need more (server-side processing, databases, CMS), we can deploy to Netlify, Vercel, or your own hosting provider. We handle the entire setup.

We guarantee 95+ across all four Lighthouse categories: Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO. Most of our sites score 98–100. We include your Lighthouse report with every delivery.

Every site is built mobile-first. We design for phones first, then scale up to tablets and desktops. Over 60% of web traffic is mobile — our sites look and perform flawlessly on every screen size.

Yes. Every build includes on-page SEO: meta titles and descriptions, Open Graph tags, structured data (JSON-LD), semantic HTML, image optimization, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, and Core Web Vitals optimization. We set you up to rank.
