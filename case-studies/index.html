Auto Repair · Cedar Park, TX · Built in 22 hours

🔴 The Problem

James had a WordPress site from 2018 that loaded in 8+ seconds. It wasn't mobile-friendly, had no booking system, and his 4.8-star Google reviews were invisible on the site. He was getting 2 online leads per month — the rest came from word of mouth.

⚡ What We Built

  • ▹Lightning-fast static site replacing bloated WordPress
  • ▹Online appointment booking with service selection
  • ▹Google Reviews widget — 4.8★ rating front and center
  • ▹Service pages optimized for "auto repair Cedar Park"
  • ▹Before/after project gallery for body work

🟢 The Result

Online leads went from 2 to 15 per month within 60 days. The booking system alone brought in $4,500/month in new revenue. Google now shows them for 12 keywords they were invisible for.
