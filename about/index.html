The Origin Story

I'm Ajaya Dahal — but most people call me AJ. I graduated from Michigan State University with a degree in engineering, then spent years at AMD as a CPU debug engineer and FPGA lead.

My job was finding defects that hide in silicon — bugs invisible to everyone else. Tracing signal paths through billions of transistors, optimizing clock cycles, ensuring zero-defect tape-outs. That's the kind of precision I was trained on.

Then I looked at the web. And I saw the same problems: slow load times nobody notices, SEO gaps bleeding revenue, mobile experiences that drive customers away, bloated code that nobody audits. Most websites are built by designers. Mine are engineered.

AjayaDesign was born from a simple idea: apply hardware-level precision to web engineering. Every site goes through the same rigorous pipeline I used to validate processors — performance benchmarks, automated testing, optimization sweeps, zero-defect deployment.
