About Us

The family story behind every magnet we make

Welcome to Ohana Magnet Co

Where we believe every picture deserves to be more than a digital memory. They deserve to be on your refrigerator or on your desk at work. We started this business as a way to get the family (our Ohana) together to do something together.

Ohana means family and no memory deserves to be left behind. We take this to heart as family has even more meaning. My wife and I attempted to have kids of our own however, we were unable to have children after multiple miscarriages. We decided to open our hearts to foster children and eventually adopted the two sweetest girls we could ever ask for.

We hope you will become part of our Ohana by allowing us to create unique and one-of-a-kind magnets for your own home or for gifts for your friends and family.

So come join our Ohana and be a part of something special!

Magnets are proudly made in Ankeny, Iowa USA. 🇺🇸
