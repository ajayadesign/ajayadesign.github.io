Why This Changes Everything

⚡

Speed = Revenue

We optimized every image, minimized CSS/JS payloads, and implemented lazy loading across all pages. Your site now loads near-instantly — critical because 53% of mobile visitors leave if a page takes over 3 seconds. Faster load times mean lower bounce rates and more completed orders.

🔍

SEO & Discoverability

We implemented advanced JSON-LD Schema markup (Product, Organization, LocalBusiness) and semantic HTML throughout. Google doesn't just read your site now — it understands your business entity, product catalog, and pricing. That means rich snippets in search results showing prices, ratings, and availability.

♿

Accessibility = Reach

A perfect Axe-Core accessibility audit means your site is WCAG AA compliant and usable by 100% of your audience, including those with visual or motor disabilities. This isn't just ethical — it's a legal safeguard and opens your store to the 26% of Americans who have some form of disability.

📱

Mobile-First = Modern

Over 70% of e-commerce traffic comes from phones. Every page was built mobile-first, then scaled up for desktop. Product grids reflow, navigation collapses to a touch-friendly hamburger menu, and CTAs are thumb-reachable. Your Shopify store was functional on mobile — now it's delightful.

🏗️

Architecture = Scale

17 pages organized with clean URL structures (/magnets/, /keychains/, /frames/, /products/...), breadcrumb navigation, and consistent design tokens. Adding new products or collections is now trivial — the design system handles it automatically.

🎯

Conversion = Growth

Strategic CTA placement above the fold, product cards with clear pricing, trust signals (family-owned badge, Made in USA), and newsletter capture forms on every page. Every design decision was made to reduce friction between "I'm browsing" and "I'm buying."

The Evolution — What We Upgraded

  • Designed a complete visual identity system with custom CSS tokens, consistent color palette (teal/terracotta/sand), and premium typography (Playfair Display + Inter)
  • Built a full-bleed hero section with layered gradient overlays and clear call-to-action above the fold
  • Created a "How It Works" three-step visual flow (Upload → We Create → You Enjoy) to demystify the ordering process
  • Designed product cards with hover animations, pricing badges, and category labels for instant scannability
  • Implemented breadcrumb navigation on all collection and product pages for better user orientation
  • Built 10 dedicated product pages with detailed specs tables, related products carousels, and prominent CTAs
  • Added structured JSON-LD markup on every page — Product schema with pricing, Organization schema, and LocalBusiness schema
  • Created a testimonial section with quote styling to build social proof and trust
  • Implemented a newsletter capture form on every page to grow the mailing list
  • Built a comprehensive footer with quick links, contact info, and social media integration (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)
  • Added keyboard-accessible dropdown navigation with Escape key support
  • Implemented skip-to-content links on every page for screen reader users
  • Ensured all color combinations pass WCAG AA contrast requirements (4.5:1 minimum)
  • Designed a mobile-first responsive grid that adapts from 375px phones to 1440px+ desktops
  • Created a contact page with client-side form validation and success feedback
  • Generated a comprehensive sitemap.xml with all 17 pages for search engine crawling
  • Added Open Graph and Twitter Card meta tags on every page for rich social media sharing
