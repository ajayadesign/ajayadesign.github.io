Instead of cold DMing, watch for these post types and comment helpfully:
1. **"Just launched my business!"** → Congrats + ask if they have a site yet
2. **"Need recommendations for X"** (where X = web-related) → Offer yourself naturally
3. **"Struggling with [marketing/online sales/getting customers]"** → Give genuine free advice, mention you build sites
4. **Business promos with no website link** → They literally need you

## Part 4: Outreach Best Practices

### ✅ Do's
- **Comment on posts first** before DMing — build familiarity
- **Give free value** in group discussions (answer questions, share tips)
- **Wait 2-3 interactions** before pitching anything
- **Reference something specific** about their business — shows you actually looked
- **Keep messages short** — under 100 words for DMs
- **Include your portfolio link** naturally, not as a pitch
- **Follow up once** after 3-5 days if no response, then move on

### ❌ Don'ts
- Don't copy-paste the same message to everyone
- Don't DM people who didn't ask for web services (use comments first)
- Don't pitch in group comments (adds value → they come to you)
- Don't spam multiple groups with the same post
- Don't be pushy or follow up more than once
- Don't disparage anyone's existing website

### 📋 Weekly Routine (15-20 min/day)
1. **Morning (5 min):** Scroll through groups, react to / comment on 3-5 posts helpfully
2. **Midday (5 min):** Search groups for keyword triggers (see Part 2)
3. **Evening (5-10 min):** Send 1-2 personalized DMs to prospects identified during the day
