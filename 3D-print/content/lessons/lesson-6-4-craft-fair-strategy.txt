CRAFT FAIR STRATEGY — SELLING IN PERSON
3D Print Academy — Module 6, Lesson 4

Your Complete Guide to Profitable In-Person Sales

Craft fairs, farmers markets, and artisan shows are the fastest way to generate revenue, build your brand, and get direct customer feedback. In-person sales let customers touch, feel, and interact with your magnetic frames, creating an unbeatable sales experience. This lesson teaches you everything from finding events to maximizing revenue per show.

FINDING AND APPLYING TO EVENTS
Search for events: Google "craft fairs near [your city]", check local Facebook groups for vendor opportunities, browse Eventbrite for artisan events, ask other local makers for recommendations. Apply 2 to 3 months ahead for popular shows. Most applications require: photos of your products, a brief bio, and a booth fee ($25 to $150 per event, typically $50). Start with smaller, free-to-vend events like farmers markets to build experience before investing in larger juried shows.

BOOTH SETUP THAT SELLS
Your booth is your store. First impression matters. Use a clean tablecloth in a neutral color that does not compete with your colorful frames. Vertical display: create an upright display board covered with magnetic sheet material so frames stick vertically at eye level. This is dramatically more effective than laying frames flat on a table. Use tiered risers to create depth and visual interest. Add a small sign with your brand name, logo, and prices.

PRODUCT DISPLAY STRATEGY
Display frames with photos inside so customers see the finished product in use. Group frames by collection: retro TV frames together, Polaroid frames together, themed sets together. Place your premium and most eye-catching piece (retro TV in avocado green) at eye level, center position. This draws customers to your booth from the aisle. Price labels should be clear, visible, and professional. Customers who have to ask prices often walk away.

THE INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCE
Let customers touch and interact with your frames. Keep a magnetic board where customers can stick and rearrange frames. Hand them frames to feel the smooth, painted finish. Let them try inserting their own phone photos (print at the booth or carry sample photos). This hands-on interaction creates emotional connection and dramatically increases purchase rates compared to a look-but-do-not-touch display.

PAYMENT PROCESSING
Accept card payments with Square or Stripe reader on your smartphone. In the age of mobile payments, cash-only booths lose significant sales. Square charges 2.6% plus $0.10 per transaction. The convenience cost is worth the additional sales you capture. Keep $20 in small bills for cash customers who need change. Display a sign showing you accept cards, cash, and mobile payments.

PRICING AND SIGNAGE AT FAIRS
Use clear pricing with tiered signage. Individual frames section with prices. Bundle deals prominently displayed with savings amount. Example sign: "Any 3 Frames for $24 (Save $6!)" or "Buy 2 Get 1 Half Off." Make the value proposition obvious without requiring mental math. Customers should immediately understand the deal. Consider a chalkboard sign for a handcrafted, artisan feel.

INVENTORY PLANNING
For a craft fair, bring: 40 to 60 individual frames across your product range, emphasizing best-sellers. 5 to 8 pre-made gift sets or bundles. Extra inventory in a box under the table. Plan for your best-case scenario: selling everything. Running out of popular items means lost revenue. Track sales at each event to improve future inventory planning. Bring 60% best-sellers, 30% variety, and 10% new designs to test market response.

BUILDING REPEAT BUSINESS
Collect customer emails at the booth with a sign-up for exclusive discounts or new design announcements. Hand out business cards with your Shopify store URL. Include a QR code linking to your online store. Follow up with a thank-you email and a first-purchase online discount. Your goal is converting craft fair customers into online customers who buy year-round.

TARGET 8 TO 12 FAIRS PER YEAR
Monthly events create consistent revenue and brand building. Rotate between locations to reach different customer bases. Track revenue per event to identify your most profitable shows. Cancel low-performing events and double down on successful ones. A well-executed craft fair schedule can generate $5000 to $15000 in annual revenue as a side hustle.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
Craft fairs are your highest-margin, fastest-revenue sales channel. Create an engaging booth with vertical magnetic displays. Let customers interact with frames. Accept card payments. Use bundle pricing to increase transaction value. Build an email list for repeat business. Target 8 to 12 events per year for consistent income.
