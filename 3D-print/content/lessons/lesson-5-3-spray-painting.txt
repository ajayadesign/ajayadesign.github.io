SPRAY PAINTING TECHNIQUE FOR MAGNET FRAMES
3D Print Academy — Module 5, Lesson 3

Professional Paint Finishes on 3D Printed Surfaces

Spray painting is the fastest and most consistent way to apply a beautiful paint finish to your magnet frames. A well-sprayed frame is indistinguishable from an injection-molded product. This lesson covers spray technique, color selection, finish types, and production efficiency for painting magnet frames.

SPRAY PAINT VS BRUSH PAINTING
Spray paint produces a smoother, more even finish than brushing. Brush strokes are visible on flat frame surfaces and look unprofessional. Spray paint atomizes the paint into a fine mist that settles onto surfaces evenly. For production volume, spray painting is also much faster — covering a frame in 30 seconds versus several minutes of careful brushing.

SPRAY PAINT TYPES
Enamel spray paint: durable, glossy or matte finish, longer cure time. Good for premium frames. Acrylic spray paint: fast drying, good adhesion to primer, wide color range. The most versatile choice. Chalk finish spray: creates a trendy matte, velvety finish without primer. Quick but less durable. Metallic spray: contains metallic flakes for gold, silver, bronze, or rose gold finishes. Premium product positioning.

THE SPRAYING TECHNIQUE
Hold the can 10 to 12 inches from the frame surface. Use sweeping back-and-forth motions at a consistent speed. Start spraying before you reach the frame edge and continue past the opposite edge. This prevents heavy paint buildup at start and stop points. Overlap each pass by about 50% for even coverage. Apply 2 to 3 light coats. A light coat appears slightly textured and not fully opaque. The second coat brings full color, and the third coat ensures uniformity.

AVOIDING COMMON PAINT MISTAKES
Runs and drips: caused by too-heavy coats or spraying too close. Always use thin coats from proper distance. Orange peel: a bumpy texture caused by painting in high humidity, cold temperatures, or from holding the can too far away. Paint at 60 to 80 degrees Fahrenheit in low humidity. Fisheyes: small craters caused by surface contamination. Ensure frames are clean and free of oils after sanding.

BETWEEN-COAT TIMING
Wait 15 to 20 minutes between coats for most spray paints. The surface should be dry to light touch but not fully cured. Painting over a flash-dried coat provides better inter-coat adhesion than painting over a fully cured surface. Check the specific paint can instructions as timing varies between brands.

COLOR STRATEGY FOR YOUR PRODUCT LINE
Core colors that sell year-round: matte white (best seller), matte black (modern and premium), metallic gold (gift market), and pastel pink (baby and gift market). Seasonal additions: holiday red and green in November, pastels for spring, earth tones for fall. Trending colors: sage green, terracotta, navy blue, dusty rose. Stock 3 to 4 core colors and rotate 1 to 2 seasonal colors for variety without excessive inventory.

METALLIC FINISHES
Metallic spray paint requires a different technique. Apply in consistent one-direction sweeps rather than back-and-forth. The metallic flakes align with the spray direction, so changing direction mid-pass creates visible banding. Apply lighter coats and let metallic paints flash for 20 to 25 minutes between coats. Metallic frames command $2 to 3 more than solid-color frames.

PRODUCTION PAINTING WORKFLOW
Batch paint all frames of the same color at once. Line up 8 to 12 primed frames on a drop cloth. Spray all frames in one session, then let the batch dry while switching to the next color. Use a rotating platform or suspend frames from wires for unobstructed spraying. This batch approach saves significant time versus painting frames individually.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
Spray painting creates professional, smooth finishes that elevate your frames from craft project to retail product. Use thin coats from 10 to 12 inches. Apply 2 to 3 coats with 15 to 20 minute dry time between. Paint in moderate temperature and humidity conditions. Batch paint by color for production efficiency. Your color selection should include core best-sellers plus rotating seasonal options.
