PRICING STRATEGY FOR MAGNET FRAMES
3D Print Academy — Module 6, Lesson 2

Maximizing Revenue Across Every Sales Channel

Pricing is both art and science. Set prices too low and you leave money on the table. Set them too high and customers walk away. This lesson teaches you a tiered pricing strategy optimized for different sales channels, frame types, and customer psychology that maximizes your revenue per frame.

THE IMPULSE BUY ZONE: $5 TO $8
At craft fairs and markets, the impulse buy threshold is approximately $5 to $10. Customers make quick buying decisions at this price point without deliberation. Price your simplest frames (small Polaroid, basic Instax Mini, single-color standard frames) at $5 to $8. These are your volume sellers. They introduce customers to your brand. Many buyers purchase 2 to 3 at this price, yielding $15 to $24 per transaction. Your profit per frame at $5 is still $3.37 (67% margin) and at $8 is $6.37 (80% margin).

THE STANDARD RANGE: $8 TO $12
Online shoppers and craft fair customers who browse carefully are comfortable in this range. Price your standard 4x3 and 4x6 frames, retro TV frames, and basic multi-piece designs here. At $10, your profit is $8.37 (84% margin). This is the bread-and-butter pricing tier that delivers the best combination of volume and margin.

THE PREMIUM TIER: $12 TO $15
Premium frames deserve premium prices. Collage frames, custom text personalization, metallic finishes, large-format frames, and gift-boxed frames all justify $12 to $15. Customers expect to pay more for larger, customized, or specially finished products. At $14, your profit is $12.37 (88% margin). Premium frames have the highest margin both in percentage and absolute dollars.

BUNDLE PRICING
Bundles increase average transaction value. Set of 3 frames: list individually at $10 each ($30 total), bundle at $24 (20% discount). Set of 5 frames: individually $50, bundle at $35 (30% discount). The customer feels they get a deal, and you move more inventory per transaction. The marginal cost of additional frames is low, so even at a discount, bundles are highly profitable.

BUY 3 GET 1 FREE PROMOTION
This classic promotion works exceptionally well for magnet frames. Customer pays for 3 frames at $10 each ($30) and receives a 4th free. Your cost for 4 frames: $6.52. Your profit: $23.48 (78% margin on the bundle). The customer perceives $10 in free value. This promotion moves inventory quickly and often converts browsers into buyers at craft fairs.

PRICE ANCHORING
Display your most expensive frames first and at eye level in your booth or on your website. When a customer sees a $15 collage frame first, an $8 standard frame feels affordable by comparison. Without anchoring, the $8 frame might feel expensive. This psychological technique is used by every successful retailer. Your display layout is part of your pricing strategy.

CHANNEL-SPECIFIC PRICING
Craft fairs: price slightly lower to account for impulse buying. Online (Shopify): price at full retail, add $5 to $8 shipping or offer free shipping over $25. Etsy: price 10% higher than Shopify to cover Etsy fees (6.5% transaction fee plus listing fees). Wholesale to boutiques: 50% of retail price (the boutique doubles to retail). Even at wholesale, your margins are strong — $5 wholesale on a $10 retail frame still yields $3.37 profit.

AVOID THE RACE TO THE BOTTOM
Do not compete on price with mass-produced magnetic frames from Amazon or dollar stores. You cannot win that price war. Instead, compete on uniqueness, quality, customization, and the story behind your product. Phrase it as small-batch artisan product, not as cheap alternative to existing frames. Customers who value unique, handmade products are willing to pay premium prices.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
Use three pricing tiers: impulse ($5-8), standard ($8-12), and premium ($12-15). Bundle frames to increase transaction value. Use price anchoring to make standard prices feel reasonable. Adjust prices by sales channel. Never compete on price alone — compete on quality, uniqueness, and brand story. Your $1.63 cost basis gives you enormous pricing flexibility.
