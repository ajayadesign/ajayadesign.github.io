COMPREHENSIVE GUIDE: Quality Control & Post-Processing for 3D Printed Magnet Photo Frames

Every frame that goes to a customer must pass rigorous quality checks. One bad frame at a craft fair or one poor Etsy review can tank your reputation. This guide covers the complete post-processing pipeline and the QC checklist every frame must pass before packaging.

SANDING TECHNIQUE — GRIT PROGRESSION:
The goal is to remove visible layer lines and create a smooth surface for painting. Skip sanding for prototypes but ALWAYS sand retail frames.

Step 1 — 120 grit (coarse):
- Removes the most prominent layer lines
- Use firm, straight strokes following the frame edges
- Focus on the front face and top edges (customer-visible surfaces)
- Time: 1-2 minutes per frame
- Tip: Hold the frame up to light at an angle to spot remaining ridges

Step 2 — 220 grit (medium):
- Smooths out the scratches from 120 grit
- Lighter pressure than step 1
- Cover all visible surfaces including sides
- Time: 1 minute per frame

Step 3 — 400 grit (fine):
- Creates a surface ready for primer
- Wet sanding recommended (keep sandpaper damp) for finer results
- Time: 30-60 seconds per frame

Step 4 — 600 grit (extra fine) — OPTIONAL:
- Only for premium frames or display pieces
- Creates near-injection-mold smoothness
- Always wet sand at this grit
- Time: 30 seconds per frame

WHAT NOT TO SAND:
- Interior photo channel (photo slides through here — sanding could make it too loose)
- Magnet recesses on the back (they're sized precisely)
- Back surface (nobody sees it, save time)
- Snap-fit clip details (sanding weakens the clip geometry)

PRIMING WITH FILLER PRIMER:
Primer serves dual purpose: fills remaining micro-gaps from sanding AND creates a uniform base for paint adhesion.

Recommended product: Rust-Oleum Filler Primer (gray) — about $6 per can = 30+ frames per can.

Application technique:
1. Hold frame on a paint stick or wire (hang from a clip attached to the back)
2. Shake can vigorously for 2 full minutes (seriously, time it)
3. Hold 6-8 inches from frame surface
4. First pass: light mist coat (should look barely gray)
5. Wait 5 minutes
6. Second pass: medium coat (full coverage, slight wet look)
7. Wait 15 minutes
8. Third pass: final coat focusing on any missed spots
9. Let dry completely: minimum 1 hour, ideally 2 hours

After primer cures: Light sand with 400 grit to knock down any primer dust nibs. This creates a glass-smooth surface. The frame should feel like ceramic at this point.

SPRAY PAINTING TECHNIQUE:
Colors that sell best (in order): Matte White, Matte Black, Rose Gold (metallic), Pastel Pink, Navy Blue, Sage Green, Natural Wood tone.

Application technique:
1. Same setup as primer — frame suspended or held on a stick
2. Shake paint can for 2 minutes
3. Start spraying OFF the frame, sweep ACROSS the frame, end OFF the frame
4. Never start or stop spraying while pointed at the frame (causes drips)
5. Apply 3-4 thin coats with 15-20 minutes between each
6. The paint should look slightly wet but not dripping after each coat
7. Final dry time: minimum 30 minutes before handling, 24 hours before clear coat

Common paint mistakes and fixes:
- Runs/drips: Sprayed too close or too much. Let dry, sand with 400 grit, re-spray.
- Orange peel: Sprayed too far away. Let dry, sand lightly, apply closer next time.
- Dust nibs: Particles landed in wet paint. Sand when dry, re-coat.
- Fisheye/craters: Contamination on surface. Sand, clean with IPA, re-prime, re-paint.

CLEAR COATING FOR DURABILITY:
The clear coat protects paint from scratches, fingerprints, and UV fading. It also adds perceived quality — customers can feel the professional finish.

Options:
- Matte clear: Modern, fingerprint-resistant, hides minor imperfections. Best seller.
- Satin clear: Slight sheen, professional look. Good middle ground.
- Gloss clear: Shiny, premium feel. Shows fingerprints more. Good for metallic paints.

Application: 2-3 light coats, same sweeping technique as paint. Dry 30 minutes between coats. FULL CURE: 24-48 hours before packaging. Frames stacked before full cure will stick together.

MAGNET INSTALLATION — TWO METHODS:

Method 1: Mid-Print Pause (Advanced, Recommended):
- In your slicer, set a pause command at the exact layer where magnet slot ceilings begin
- Printer pauses mid-print → you drop magnets into the open slots → resume print
- The next layers print OVER the magnets, fully encapsulating them
- Pros: Strongest possible hold, invisible from outside, no glue needed, professional result
- Cons: Requires accurate pause timing, can't fix wrong polarity after resuming
- Critical: CHECK POLARITY BEFORE DROPPING IN. Use a marked reference magnet.
- Cura: Install "Pause at Height" plugin → set to the correct layer
- PrusaSlicer: Edit G-code at layer → add M0 (pause) command

Method 2: Post-Print Glue (Easy, Beginner-Friendly):
- Print frame with open magnet recesses on the back
- Apply 1 small drop of cyanoacrylate (super glue) to each recess
- Press magnet in with correct polarity — hold for 30 seconds
- Optional: apply adhesive felt pad over magnets for a clean look
- Pros: Easy, forgiving (can pry out and reglue wrong polarity)
- Cons: Magnets slightly visible from back, glue can be messy

POLARITY CHECK PROCEDURE:
1. Get one reference magnet — mark the "fridge side" with a Sharpie dot
2. Before inserting each magnet, touch it to the reference
3. The side that REPELS the marked side = the side facing INTO the frame (away from fridge)
4. The side that ATTRACTS = the side facing OUT toward the fridge
5. All magnets in all frames should be oriented the same way
6. Test: finished frame should snap firmly to any fridge/metal surface

PRE-SHIP QC CHECKLIST — Every frame MUST pass ALL checks:

STRUCTURAL CHECKS:
□ Frame is flat (no warping) — place on flat surface, should not rock
□ No visible cracks or layer separation along edges
□ Photo window dimensions correct — test fit with actual photo of that size
□ Photo slides in and out smoothly with slight friction (not too loose, not too tight)
□ Frame edges are smooth — no sharp burrs, blobs, or rough patches
□ Frame border width is consistent all the way around

MAGNET CHECKS:
□ All magnets installed with correct polarity — test on a fridge or metal surface
□ Magnets are secure — shake test, no rattling or movement
□ Magnets sit flush or slightly recessed (will not scratch fridge surface)
□ Frame holds on fridge without sliding down (minimum 4 magnets for 4x6+)
□ Frame holds on fridge with photo inserted (weight test with actual photo)
□ Frame holds on fridge with slight downward tug (durability check)

FINISH CHECKS (for painted frames):
□ No bare spots or missed primer/paint areas
□ No paint runs, drips, or sags
□ Even color coverage — check in good lighting at multiple angles
□ Clear coat applied and fully cured (24-48 hours minimum)
□ No fingerprints embedded in the finish
□ No dust nibs or particles in the clear coat
□ Color matches the product listing photo

FINAL CHECKS:
□ Back cover fits properly — snaps on and off without excessive force
□ Snap-fit clips engage with a satisfying click and hold firmly
□ Back cover sits flush (no gaps or rocking)
□ Overall appearance is "gift-worthy" — would YOU pay $10-15 for this?
□ Frame weight feels right — substantial but not heavy
□ No loose threads of filament (trim any stringing remnants)

REJECTION CRITERIA — REPRINT, DO NOT SELL:
✗ Visible layer shifts (indicates printer mechanical issue)
✗ Elephant's foot on base affecting flatness
✗ Stringing that cannot be cleanly removed by hand
✗ Wrong magnet polarity (embedded magnets can't be fixed)
✗ Frame cracked during post-processing (sanding or painting)
✗ Under-extrusion gaps visible in walls
✗ Warping that can't be corrected
✗ Paint quality below your standard samples

TIME BUDGET PER FRAME (PRODUCTION PACE):
| Step | Active Time | Wait Time |
|------|------------|-----------|
| Sanding (120→400 grit) | 3 min | — |
| Primer (3 coats) | 2 min spray | 1 hr dry |
| Light sand post-primer | 30 sec | — |
| Paint (3-4 coats) | 2 min spray | 30 min between + 24 hr final |
| Clear coat (2-3 coats) | 2 min spray | 30 min between + 24 hr cure |
| Magnet install | 2 min | — |
| QC check | 1 min | — |
| Packaging | 1 min | — |
| TOTAL ACTIVE | ~13 min | ~26 hr (mostly curing) |

The key insight: batch your post-processing! Sand 20 frames, then prime 20, then while those dry, sand the next 20. Assembly-line approach = maximum efficiency. You can post-process 50+ frames per day with just 2-3 hours of active work.
