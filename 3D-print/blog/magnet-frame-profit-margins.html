Hidden Costs People Forget

Let's be honest about the costs that don't show up in the per-frame math:

  • Failed prints: Budget 5-10% waste when starting, drops to 1-2% with experience
  • Photography: Good product photos are essential. A $50 light box pays for itself immediately
  • Shipping supplies: $0.50-1.00 per order (bubble mailers, tape)
  • Etsy ads: Optional but effective. Start at $1-5/day
  • Your time: Post-processing, listing, packing — factor in your hourly value

Even with these, margins stay above 75% in most scenarios. Compare that to dropshipping (10-30%) or handmade crafts (40-60%).

Why Margins Stay High

Three structural advantages keep magnet frame margins consistently high:

  • Raw material cost floor: PLA is a commodity — prices trend down, not up
  • No minimum orders: Print 1 or 100, same unit cost
  • Design moat: Your original designs can't be easily replicated by mass manufacturers
