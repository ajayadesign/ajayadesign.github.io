Value Post

Facebook Groups
Quick tip that tripled my magnet frame sales 📈 I used to sell plain frames for $5 each and barely got orders. Then I started offering: ✅ Custom colors to match kitchen/nursery decor ✅ Holiday-themed frames (Christmas, Valentine's, etc.) ✅ Wedding/event bulk packages (10+ frames at a discount) ✅ Name personalization for $2 extra Now my average order is $25+ and I'm getting repeat customers. The key insight? People don't buy "a magnet frame" — they buy a gift, a memory display, a decoration. Price and position accordingly. Anyone else finding that customization is the key to higher margins? What add-ons work best for you?
