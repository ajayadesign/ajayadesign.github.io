// FAQ

Common Questions

What 3D printer do I need?

Any FDM printer works! Popular choices include the Ender 3, Prusa i3 MK3S, Bambu Lab A1 Mini, or any similar printer. Module 1 covers printer selection in detail so you can pick the best option for your budget.

How much can I earn?

Magnet frames sell for $15–$30 each, and materials cost just $1–$3 per frame. That's a 5–30× markup. Print 10–20 frames a day and you're looking at $150–$600/day in revenue.

Do I need design experience?

No! We start from absolute zero in TinkerCAD (free, browser-based). No prior CAD, design, or 3D printing experience required. By Module 3, you'll be designing advanced multi-piece frames on your own.

What's included in the course?

43 video lessons across 7 modules (16+ hours), a 15+ STL file library with commercial license, slicer profiles, a pricing calculator, and access to our private community. The mentorship tier adds 3 live 1-on-1 video sessions with AJ.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes! Try the first 7 lessons (the entire first module). If you're not satisfied, we'll give you a full refund — no questions asked. We're that confident in the content.

What software do I need?

All software used is free: TinkerCAD (browser-based), Fusion 360 (free for personal use), and Cura or PrusaSlicer (open source). No purchases needed.

Can I sell the magnet frames I make?

Absolutely. All STL files come with a commercial license. You're free to print, modify, and sell the finished products. Module 6 teaches you exactly how to build a profitable business around them.
