📹 Video Script Templates

5 proven formats. Copy the script, shoot the video, post it. Rinse and repeat.

TIMELAPSE FORMAT

"Watch me 3D print a magnetic photo frame in real time"

🎵 [Use trending timelapse sound]

HOOK (0-3s): Start with the printer already running. Text overlay: "Watch me 3D print a magnetic photo frame ⏱️"

BODY (3-45s): Timelapse of the full print. Show the bed heating up → first layer going down → frame taking shape → finished print popping off the bed. Speed up 10-20x.

REVEAL (45-55s): Slow down to real time. Peel the frame off the bed. Snap in the magnets (satisfying click sound!). Slide in a photo.

CTA (55-60s): Text overlay: "Want the files? Link in bio 👆" Hold the finished frame next to a fridge with photos already on it.

💡 TIP: Film in good lighting. The timelapse does the work — no talking needed. Great for your first video.

STORY FORMAT

"POV: you just started a 3D printing business for $200"

🎵 [Use motivational/aspirational sound — "original sound" storytelling works great]

HOOK (0-3s): You, sitting at your desk looking at your printer. Text: "POV: you just started a 3D printing business for $200"

SCENE 1 (3-10s): Show unboxing/setting up a printer. Text: "Week 1: bought an Ender 3 on sale"

SCENE 2 (10-18s): Show first failed print, then first good print. Text: "Week 2: figured out how to not make spaghetti"

SCENE 3 (18-28s): Show printing multiple frames, packaging them. Text: "Week 3: listed on Etsy… first sale 🤯"

SCENE 4 (28-38s): Show Etsy notification sounds, multiple orders. Text: "Week 4: 15 sales this week"

SCENE 5 (38-45s): Show your setup now — organized, multiple prints going. Text: "Month 3: this pays my car payment"

CTA (45-50s): Face camera. "Want to learn how? I'll teach you everything." Point up. Link in bio.

💡 TIP: Use quick cuts, each scene 3-5 seconds max. This format crushes because people love transformation stories.

INCOME REVEAL FORMAT

"I make $X/day selling these on Etsy"

🎵 [Money/cash register sounds or trending "money" audio]

HOOK (0-3s): Hold up a magnet frame. Text: "This little thing makes me $XX/day" (use your real number!)

BREAKDOWN (3-15s): Show your Etsy dashboard (blur sensitive info). Text overlay the math:

"Cost to make: $0.12 filament + $0.15 magnets + $0.10 electricity = $0.37"

"I sell for: $12"

"Profit per frame: $11.63"

SCALE (15-25s): "I print 8 per day while I sleep/work/study" Show printer running with batch of frames.

PROOF (25-35s): Quick flash of Etsy sales notifications. "That's $XX/week from a $200 printer"

CTA (35-40s): "Comment 'HOW' and I'll send you the breakdown" (drives engagement!)

💡 TIP: Be honest with your numbers. Transparency builds trust and drives WAY more engagement than inflated claims.

TUTORIAL FORMAT

"How to make magnetic photo frames (step by step)"

🎵 [Chill lo-fi or "how to" trending sound]

HOOK (0-3s): Hold finished frame on fridge. "Here's how I make these magnetic photo frames everyone's been asking about"

STEP 1 (3-10s): Show slicing software. "First, I slice the STL file. 0.2mm layer height, 15% infill, no supports needed"

STEP 2 (10-20s): Show printer starting. "Hit print. This one takes about 45 minutes." Quick timelapse of print.

STEP 3 (20-30s): Remove from bed. "Pop it off, clean up any stringing." Show quick cleanup.

STEP 4 (30-40s): "Press in the magnets — 6mm neodymium, $0.15 for a pack." Show satisfying magnet snap.

STEP 5 (40-50s): "Slide in your photo and stick it on the fridge." Show the final result, maybe rearrange a few frames.

CTA (50-60s): "Want the exact files and settings? Link in bio. I teach you everything from zero to selling on Etsy."

💡 TIP: Tutorial content gets saved and shared the most. These are slow burners that keep getting views for months.

COST BREAKDOWN FORMAT

"This $0.12 frame sells for $12 on Etsy"

🎵 [Dramatic/reveal sound — "oh no oh no" or cash register works]

HOOK (0-3s): Hold up frame next to a penny. Text: "This costs less to make than this penny"

COST 1 (3-8s): Show filament spool. Text overlay: "Filament: $0.12" — weigh the frame on a kitchen scale for proof.

COST 2 (8-13s): Show bag of magnets. Text: "Magnets: $0.15" — pour out the bulk bag, show how many you get.

COST 3 (13-18s): Show electricity meter/printer running. Text: "Electricity: ~$0.10"

TOTAL (18-23s): Big text: "Total cost: $0.37" — pause for effect.

REVEAL (23-30s): Show Etsy listing. Text: "Sells for: $12.00" Show the 100x markup visual.

TWIST (30-38s): "And I can print 8 of these while I'm at school/work/sleeping." Show batch on print bed.

CTA (38-42s): "Want to start? Everything you need is in my bio 👆"

💡 TIP: The "pennies to dollars" format is TikTok gold. People can't resist watching cost breakdowns. Film one of these first!

📅 Posting Schedule Guide

When, how often, and what to post for maximum reach.

🕐

Best Times to Post

  • ▸ 7-9 AM — morning scroll
  • ▸ 12-1 PM — lunch break
  • ▸ 7-9 PM — evening wind-down
  • ▸ 10 PM — late night browsing

Post in YOUR audience's timezone. US-based? Aim for EST/CST.

🔄

Frequency

  • ▸ Minimum: 1 post/day
  • ▸ Sweet spot: 1-2 posts/day
  • ▸ Growth mode: 2-3 posts/day

Consistency > volume. Better to post 1/day for 30 days than 5/day for a week.

🎯

Content Mix

Value / Tutorial 60%
Behind the scenes 30%
Promotional 10%
