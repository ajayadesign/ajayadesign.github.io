# 60-Second Course Trailer Script

> **Format**: Vertical (9:16) for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
> **Duration**: 55–60 seconds
> **Tone**: Energetic but real. Not salesy — show, don't tell.
> **Music**: Lo-fi beat or light electronic (royalty-free)

---

## SHOT LIST & SCRIPT

### [0:00–0:03] HOOK — Close-up shot
**Visual**: Extreme close-up of a 3D printer nozzle laying down the first layer. The frame outline starts forming.
**Text overlay**: "I turned a $200 printer into a business."
**Audio**: Printer whirring sound, then music drops in

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### What you need to film:
1. **Printer close-up** — First layer being laid down (use phone macro mode)
2. **Print time-lapse** — One full frame print, phone on tripod (OctoLapse or just 8x speed)
3. **Product shots** — 3-4 different frame styles on a fridge. Natural lighting. Family photos inside.
4. **Photo insertion** — Satisfying clip of sliding a Polaroid into the slot
5. **Hands unboxing** — Tissue paper, frame reveal
6. **Screen recordings** — TinkerCAD, Cura slicer, student portal dashboard, pricing calculator

### What you can generate with AI:
- **Music**: Suno.ai or free YouTube Audio Library
- **Text overlays**: CapCut (free) or DaVinci Resolve
- **Mockup Shopify notifications**: Screenshot + animate in CapCut

### Estimated filming time: 2–3 hours
### Estimated editing time: 1–2 hours in CapCut

### Platforms to post:
- Instagram Reels (primary — link in bio)
- TikTok (repurpose)
- YouTube Shorts (repurpose)
- Pinterest Idea Pins (repurpose)
