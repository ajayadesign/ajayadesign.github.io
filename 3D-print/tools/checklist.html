✅ QC Checklist

Check every item before selling or shipping a magnet frame.

Print Quality

No visible layer lines on front face

Front-facing surfaces should be smooth at arm's length

No stringing between features

Clean travel moves, no cobwebs or wisps

No warping or lifting on base

Frame sits flat on a table, no corner curl

No elephant's foot on bottom layer

Bottom edge should be clean, not bulging

Consistent color throughout

No discoloration, white streaks, or temperature artifacts

Dimensional Accuracy

Photo insert fits correctly

Test with target photo size (Instax/Polaroid/4×6) — should slide in without force

Photo doesn't fall out

Gentle shake test — photo should stay held by friction or clips

Magnet slots are correct depth

6×2mm neodymium magnets should sit flush or slightly recessed

Magnet Installation

All magnets installed

Count matches design spec (usually 4 per frame)

Polarity is correct

Frame sticks firmly to fridge/metal surface — test all 4 corners

Magnets are secure (no rattle)

Shake test — magnets should not move, click, or fall out

Glue is clean (no overflow)

If post-glued: no visible glue residue around magnet wells

Finishing (if painted)

Even paint coverage

No thin spots, drips, or orange peel texture

Clear coat applied

Scratch resistance test — rub with fingernail, no damage

No paint in magnet wells or photo slot

Photo still slides in, magnets still work through paint layer

Final Check

No sharp edges

Run finger along all edges — smooth, no snagging

Overall appearance: would YOU buy this?

The "fridge test" — put it on your own fridge with a real photo. Does it look premium?

Packaged and labeled

In protective packaging, ready for shipping or sale
