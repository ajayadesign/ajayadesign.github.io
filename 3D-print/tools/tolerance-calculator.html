❓ How to Measure Your Printer Offset
  1. Print a 20mm calibration cube at your normal settings.
  2. Measure X and Y with calipers. Example: if it measures 20.15mm, your offset is +0.15.
  3. If it measures 19.8mm, your offset is -0.20.
  4. Enter the average of X and Y into "Printer Offset" above.
  5. Choose a safety margin based on your confidence level.
