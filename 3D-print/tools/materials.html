🛒 Materials List

Everything you need to start making and selling magnet photo frames.

ESSENTIALMust-have to start RECOMMENDEDBig quality boost OPTIONALNice to have

Filament

PLA+ Filament (1.75mm)

ESSENTIAL

The go-to for magnet frames. Strong, easy to print, low warp. Recommend eSun PLA+ or Polymaker PolyTerra.

~$18–25/kg ~40 frames per kg

PETG Filament (1.75mm)

OPTIONAL

More heat resistant and flexible than PLA. Good for outdoor or car dashboard frames.

~$20–28/kg Harder to tune

Silk PLA (metallic)

RECOMMENDED

Premium look with shiny metallic finish. Gold, silver, rose gold are top sellers. Great for gift/bridal frames.

~$22–30/kg Higher perceived value

Magnets

6×2mm Neodymium Magnets

ESSENTIAL

N52 grade. Small but incredibly strong. 4 per frame holds securely on any fridge.

~$8–12 / 100 pcs 25 frames per pack

8×3mm Neodymium Magnets

OPTIONAL

Stronger hold for larger frames or heavier designs. Use 2-4 per frame.

~$10–15 / 100 pcs For large frames

Post-Processing

Sandpaper Variety Pack

RECOMMENDED

220, 400, 600 grit. Wet-sand between paint coats for a glass-smooth finish.

~$8–12 Lasts 50+ frames

Filler Primer Spray

RECOMMENDED

Fills layer lines before painting. Rustoleum Filler Primer is the gold standard.

~$6–10 15+ frames per can

Spray Paint (assorted)

OPTIONAL

Montana Gold or Rustoleum 2X for solid coverage. Black, white, and metallics are most popular.

~$5–8/can 10+ frames per can

Clear Coat (gloss/matte)

RECOMMENDED

Protects paint and gives professional finish. UV-resistant versions prevent yellowing.

~$6–10 15+ frames per can

Super Glue (CA glue)

ESSENTIAL

For securing magnets into slots. Gorilla Super Glue Gel is best — no dripping. Loctite 401 also works.

~$5–8 100+ frames per tube

Tools

Digital Calipers

ESSENTIAL

Measure slot dimensions, magnet fit, and photo tolerances. 6-inch digital is perfect.

~$12–25 One-time purchase

Print Removal Spatula

ESSENTIAL

Thin, flexible spatula for removing prints without damage. Usually comes with your printer.

~$5–10 If not included

Flush Cutters / Nippers

RECOMMENDED

Clean up stringing and support remnants. Sharp, fine-tipped flush cutters are best.

~$6–12 One-time purchase

Tweezers (fine tip)

RECOMMENDED

Place magnets precisely into small slots. Anti-static tweezers are great for this.

~$5–8 One-time purchase

Deburring Tool

OPTIONAL

Quickly shave brim edges and clean up slot openings. Faster than sandpaper for rough cleanup.

~$8–12 One-time purchase

Packaging & Shipping

Bubble Mailers (6×9")

ESSENTIAL

Perfect for single magnet frames. Lightweight keeps shipping under $4.

~$12 / 50 pcs $0.24 per frame

Tissue Paper / Crinkle

RECOMMENDED

Wrap each frame before putting in mailer. Prevents scratches and adds a premium unboxing feel.

~$8 / 100 sheets $0.08 per frame

Thank You Cards

RECOMMENDED

Business-card-size "thank you" with socials and review link. Boosts repeat customers and reviews.

~$15 / 100 pcs $0.15 per card

Branded Stickers

OPTIONAL

Logo sticker to seal the packaging. Small touch that elevates brand perception.

~$12 / 100 pcs $0.12 per sticker
